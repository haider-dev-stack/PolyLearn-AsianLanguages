二人でお願いしますfutari de onegaishimasu
Table for two, please💡 Staff often greet you with 何名様ですか？ (nanmei-sama desu ka?) — “How many people?”
メニューをお願いしますmenyuu o onegaishimasu
The menu, please
おすすめは何ですか？osusume wa nan desu ka?
What do you recommend?
これをくださいkore o kudasai
I'll have this, please💡 Point at the menu or the plastic food display as you say it.
辛くしないでくださいkaraku shinaide kudasai
Not spicy, please
ベジタリアンですbejitarian desu
I'm vegetarian
もう一つお願いしますmou hitotsu onegaishimasu
One more, please
いただきますitadakimasu
Let's eat (said before eating)💡 Said with hands together before your first bite.
ごちそうさまでしたgochisousama deshita
Thank you for the meal💡 Say it to staff as you leave — they will love it.
お会計お願いしますokaikei onegaishimasu
The bill, please💡 Tipping is not expected in Japan.
カードで払えますか？kaado de haraemasu ka?
Can I pay by card?
