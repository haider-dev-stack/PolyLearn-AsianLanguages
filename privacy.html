PolyLearn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. PolyLearn (“the app”) is a language-learning app for Japanese, Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean and Thai. You must be 13 or older to create an account.

What stays on your device

Without an account, your progress, settings, theme, name, bio and profile photo are stored only on your device.

Accounts (optional)

If you create an account we store your email address (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase) and a copy of your progress, settings, nickname, bio and profile photo so you can restore them on another device. We use this only to run the app. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Leaderboard (optional)

If you choose “Join the ranking”, your display name, profile photo, learning language and XP totals are visible to other learners. Names and bios are checked automatically for abusive language. Other users can report or block a profile; reported profiles are reviewed and may be removed. You can leave the leaderboard at any time in Settings, which removes your entry.

AI tutor

Messages you type to the AI tutor are sent to our server to generate a reply and are filtered for abusive language. Do not enter personal information.

Microphone and speech

If you use voice input, your device’s speech service may process audio under its own privacy policy. PolyLearn does not record or store audio.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, saved progress and leaderboard entry.
By email: write to Haiderleohosni2013@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, using the subject “Delete my PolyLearn account”, and we will delete it within 30 days. We may keep minimal records only where the law requires it.

Children

PolyLearn is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
